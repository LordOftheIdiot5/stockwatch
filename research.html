Instrument identity architecture

From listing to persistent identifier

01 / IDENTIFY

Listing evidence

Exchange MIC plus local ticker, or a source-backed ISIN, forms a mapping request. Symbols remain exchange-specific.

02 / MAP

OpenFIGI identifiers

Store candidate instrument FIGI, share-class FIGI and composite FIGI separately. Ambiguous or missing matches remain unresolved.

03 / RETAIN

Dated evidence

Record actual observed mapping changes. Preserve earlier candidate evidence during outages without implying historical assignments before collection.

History and analytical use

A/B indicators retain one observed ratio per UTC scan day, not reconstructed equity prices. A/B comparisons require distinct share classes, with attention to voting rights, liquidity and currency.

Company news is retained as source-linked metadata, not republished articles. Licensed daily closes require source, entitlement, currency, adjustment status and actual observation dates.

Reuse and traceability

OpenFIGI reference metadata is distinct from vendor quotes. Public display, storage and commercial redistribution permissions must be established for each source. The Leivro data platform is our intended central catalog; shared commercial infrastructure remains under development.

OpenFIGI facilitator listing is not yet confirmed and no Bloomberg endorsement is claimed.
