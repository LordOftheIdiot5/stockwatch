THE METHODOLOGY

What these signals mean

Price change. Percentage movement from the prior closing price. Prices are sourced from Yahoo's chart endpoint and are not tick-by-tick market quotes.

Unusual activity. Daily move compared with the watchlist median and each stock's own volatility; relative volume compared with its 20-day median. Thinly traded shares are not volume-alerted.

News & filings. Company disclosures and selected news links are grouped by stock. Automated matching and translation can make errors; verify important items against the original source.

No predictive claim. Historical backtesting did not establish a reliable trading edge from the price and volume thresholds.
