    Not advice, and not a view on where anything is going.

    The price and volume thresholds have now been measured against two years of daily bars, entering at the close after each signal: they do not predict returns. Two of twenty-four signal-and-horizon pairs beat the baseline over the full period, which is about what twenty-four tests produce by chance — and both vanished when the period was split in half, appearing only in the first year. A control that peeked at the future showed up at +17%, so the measurement works; there is simply nothing there. The method, and the numbers. So this is a tool for noticing things quickly and reading them in one place — which is what the filings and the news are for. It is not an edge, and the volume column is not a forecast.
