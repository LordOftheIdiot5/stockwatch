# News archive recovery — V89c phase 1
Git month partitions are the durable source of source-linked news metadata. The normal scan sends the previous seven days to Cloudflare D1. The separate recovery workflow validates and replays all retained IDs on first deployment, weekly, or manual dispatch. The existing Worker upserts by ID, so replays do not duplicate rows and can recover older missing IDs. Batches must be acknowledged. A missing endpoint/token or failed request makes the recovery job visibly fail; it does not interfere with normal scans. The aggregate health count is diagnostic, not proof of exact database parity. Exact ID readback and archive coverage metrics remain follow-up work. No new provider collection, raw article bodies or commercial redistribution rights are introduced. As history grows, monitor workflow runtime and move to checkpointed partitions before the timeout becomes limiting.
