## Technical and forecasting contract
Observation: source/native ID, locator, entity/listing/asset/SKU/geo ID, metric, unit, currency, period start/end, published/retrieved/revised time, vintage, quality, source rights (collect/retain/display/export independently), checksum. Stable logical identity and immutable revisions; never confuse ingestion and observation. Signals reference exact inputs/baselines, algorithm version, comparison basis, freshness, scope and limitations. Separate measured fact, hypothesis, validated exposure, scenario and forecast. Compute centrally once per eligible vintage; versioned read-only outputs, no restricted raw data in public bundles. Private credentials isolated.
Wallenius pilot: higher net freight +4.9%, volume -2.9%, backlog -10.8% are mixed operating indicators, not a stock call. Validate period definitions, contract timing, fleet/mix, costs, valuation, surprises and benchmark-adjusted total returns before estimating direction. Energy pilot: compare reservoir levels to seasonal history and use as-of weather/snow, demand, outages/flows; distinguish measured vs forecast. Leivro pilot: authorized exact regional offers and energy-use ratings, tariffs, lifecycle and availability; distinguish quote from estimated ownership cost. No fake scores or probabilities. Store forecasts before outcomes; compare with naive/persistence and seasonal baselines, rolling held-out tests, calibration, uncertainty intervals, coverage, errors and failure regimes. No publication of predictive claims until criteria documented and met.
## Permanent data acquisition and retention policy (owner direction 2026-11-24)
N02/N04/N09 are continuous operating obligations, not one-off completed tasks. Every new source integration must identify collection, private retention, public display and commercial export rights independently. Archive every eligible successful source vintage and normalized observation/revision at the authorized cadence; preserve source-native observation/publication time, retrieval receipt, exact identity, unit, provenance and checksum. Deduplicate identical content, retain retrieval evidence, never overwrite history or promote restricted data. Where retention is not authorized, record the permitted metadata and blocker, not prohibited raw bytes. Use private R2 for immutable original content and D1 for indexed observations, source registry, archive pointers and ingestion receipts; use existing domain archives where operational, not a second redundant collector. Public GitHub/Pages snapshots are projections, not proof of durable archival. Verify actual writes, index consistency, replay, restoration, coverage, quotas and costs; distinguish code, configured service and proven operational archive. Collect only at source-approved cadence; bounded retries and last-known dated fallback. Every development session must review source coverage gaps and advance at least one unblocked source-discovery or retention action when practicable. Maintain a candidate register with provider, source URL, datasets, geography, cadence, history, rights evidence, cost, priority and review status. Prefer primary official/open commercial sources, seek explicit permission when needed; do not buy or enable paid sources without owner approval. Track new eligible observations, distinct source vintages, failed writes, coverage, last valid fetch and restoration tests by domain. Preserve data lineage for model backtests; no retrospective knowledge leakage. Automated discovery may propose sources but cannot silently authorize ingestion or publication.
