## Technical and forecasting contract
Observation: source/native ID, locator, entity/listing/asset/SKU/geo ID, metric, unit, currency, period start/end, published/retrieved/revised time, vintage, quality, source rights (collect/retain/display/export independently), checksum. Stable logical identity and immutable revisions; never confuse ingestion and observation. Signals reference exact inputs/baselines, algorithm version, comparison basis, freshness, scope and limitations. Separate measured fact, hypothesis, validated exposure, scenario and forecast. Compute centrally once per eligible vintage; versioned read-only outputs, no restricted raw data in public bundles. Private credentials isolated.
Wallenius pilot: higher net freight +4.9%, volume -2.9%, backlog -10.8% are mixed operating indicators, not a stock call. Validate period definitions, contract timing, fleet/mix, costs, valuation, surprises and benchmark-adjusted total returns before estimating direction. Energy pilot: compare reservoir levels to seasonal history and use as-of weather/snow, demand, outages/flows; distinguish measured vs forecast. Leivro pilot: authorized exact regional offers and energy-use ratings, tariffs, lifecycle and availability; distinguish quote from estimated ownership cost. No fake scores or probabilities. Store forecasts before outcomes; compare with naive/persistence and seasonal baselines, rolling held-out tests, calibration, uncertainty intervals, coverage, errors and failure regimes. No publication of predictive claims until criteria documented and met.
